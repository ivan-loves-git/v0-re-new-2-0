*Platform Overview:*
Wave 1.0 automates our entire candidate evaluation workflow from CV to final assessment. When a candidate submits their CV and email, multiple AI agents work in parallel to extract professional history, enrich profiles with web research, and score against Re-New's criteria across five dimensions: commercial experience, financial sophistication, management capability, sustainability alignment, and leadership potential. The whole process takes under 5 minutes per candidate.

The system generates personalized outreach emails that reference specific candidate strengths and Re-New connections, boosting our brand perception while allowing to manage, potentially, hundreds of candidate per campaign. Candidates who pass the initial threshold receive tailored follow-up requests for additional information, creating a progressive profiling approach rather than demanding lengthy upfront forms.

Every interaction – calls, meetings, emails, notes – gets logged and automatically processed by AI to continuously enrich candidate profiles. This means we never lose context or intelligence gathered throughout the assessment process. The platform tracks all assessment costs and provides real-time analytics on our candidate pipeline, conversion rates, and financial metrics.

The result is a system that can process 10x our current candidate volume while maintaining personalized engagement, consistent scoring, and complete visibility into our recruitment pipeline.
