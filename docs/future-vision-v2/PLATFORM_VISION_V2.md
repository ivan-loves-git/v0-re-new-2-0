## 1. The Market Opportunity

### The French SME Succession Crisis

**370,000 French SMEs** plan to transfer ownership by 2030 (BPI France study). At current rates, only 130,000 will successfully find buyers. **200,000 businesses risk failing to find a successor.** 3 million jobs are at stake.

The problem isn't supply. There are plenty of businesses to buy. The problem is **buyer readiness**:
- 70-75% of all M&A deals fail to create value (Fortune, 40,000 deals analyzed)
- **90% of first-time small business buyers fail** in their first attempt
- Average deal timeline: 12-18 months, most buyers underestimate by 6+ months

### Why Buyers Fail: 7 Systemic Readiness Gaps

| # | Gap | Impact |
|---|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Financial skills gap** | Can't evaluate deals or structure financing |
| 2 | **Negotiation inexperience** | Emotionally driven, not strategically |
| 3 | **Emotional decision-making** | "Falling in love" with a target, ignoring red flags |
| 4 | **Timeline underestimation** | Typical deal takes 12-18 months, not 3-6 |
| 5 | **Insufficient advisory team** | No lawyer, no accountant, no M&A advisor |
| 6 | **No integration plan** | Buy the business, then figure it out |
| 7 | **Isolation** | No peer network, no coaching during the process |

These gaps are **systemic and predictable**. They can be measured, addressed, and verified. That's what Re-New does.

### The Infrastructure Gap

The French acquisition ecosystem already has:
- **Deal sourcing platforms**: BPI Transmission (45,000+ listings), Transentreprise (6,000+), Fusacq (2,000+), CessionPME (118,000), Alvo
- **Training programs**: CRA (3-week flagship, 37 years of history), CCI ("5 jours pour reprendre"), ALTHEO
- **Government support**: BPI France (financing guarantees, matching, Pass Repreneur), CCI programs
- **Institutional CRMs**: DealCloud, Midaxo (designed for PE firms, not individual buyers)

What does **NOT** exist anywhere:
1. A comprehensive **repreneur journey management platform** from ideation to first 100 days
2. A **competence development dashboard** showing readiness evolution over time
3. A **certification system** that makes buyer readiness visible and verifiable to sellers
4. An **integrated platform** combining assessment, preparation, deal sourcing, and coaching

**This is Re-New's white space.**

## 2. Re-New's Strategic Position

### What Re-New Already Has (And Competitors Don't)

#### Process Mastery Encoded in Software

The 3-tier assessment system isn't just scoring. It's a structured framework that:
- Separates profile quality (WHO: 0-100) from project maturity (WHEN: 0-100)
- Uses financial triangulation to detect incoherence (ambition vs. means)
- Flags logical contradictions (5 warning flags that override scores)
- Maps 11 readiness milestones across 4 journey stages
- Provides automatic recommendations (deal_flow / priority_interview / interview / starter_pack)

No training program, no deal platform, no government agency does this.

#### The Dual Scoring Innovation

Traditional M&A qualification asks "is this buyer good?" Re-New asks two separate questions:
- **WHO**: Can this person execute? (experience, leadership, crisis management, investment decisions)
- **WHEN**: Is the project viable? (deal size vs. equity vs. structure coherence)

A brilliant operator with no financial plan gets different guidance than a well-funded novice. This nuance is Re-New's intellectual property.

#### The Journey-Centric Data Model

The platform already tracks repreneurs across:
- **Lifecycle stages**: lead / qualified / client
- **Journey stages**: explorer / learner / ready / serial_acquirer
- **11 binary milestones** across 4 journey categories

This is the foundation for a certification architecture, not just a CRM.

### What Re-New Doesn't Have Yet

- No repreneur-facing portal (repreneurs can't see their own data)
- No content library (knowledge is in Bertrand's head)
- No payment processing
- No certification display or verification system
- No AI-powered tools for repreneurs
- No seller/intermediary integration

## 3. The Platform Vision

### Concept: Curated Acceleration Platform

Re-New becomes the **quality assurance layer** in the French acquisition market.

**For repreneurs**: "Re-New is where I go to become a serious, certified buyer. The platform shows me exactly where I am in my journey, what I need to learn next, what milestones I'm missing, and connects me with the right resources and deals when I'm ready."

**For sellers/intermediaries** (V2.0+, manual at first): "When I see a Re-New certified repreneur, I know they've been vetted, scored, coached, and verified. I trust them because Re-New's certification means something concrete: validated financing, documented industry expertise, completed preparation."

**For Re-New**: "We own the buyer side of the French SME acquisition market. Every repreneur in our platform is tracked, scored, and guided. We monetize through subscriptions, coaching, and deal commissions."

### How It Works

```
1. INTAKE (public)
   Repreneur fills questionnaire
   -> automatic WHO/WHEN scoring + warning flags

2. GATE (team decision)
   Minimum score threshold
   -> accepted or redirected to resources

3. ONBOARDING (the moment of value)
   Personalized intelligence message showing Re-New "gets it"
   -> "Based on your profile, you're at [stage]. Your strengths: [X,Y].
      Your gaps: [A,B]. Here's what to focus on next."

4. PORTAL (repreneur-facing)
   - Journey map showing position and progress
   - Next recommended actions
   - Competence radar chart (6-dimension readiness)
   - Milestone tracking with certification badges
   - Resource library (gated by tier)
   - AI tools (gated by credits/tier)

5. DEVELOPMENT (ongoing)
   - Self-paced resources (free tier)
   - Coaching sessions (paid)
   - Certification exams/validations (automated + human)
   - Community/peer network

6. DEAL FLOW (for qualified repreneurs)
   - Access to acquisition opportunities
   - AI-powered matching based on profile + preferences
   - Re-New facilitation + commission on closed deals
```

**What Karim feels**: "Wait. They actually read my answers. They spotted the financing gap I was worried about. And they have a 13-stage framework? These people know what they're doing." He bookmarks the email. He checks the resource links. He's already thinking about Re-New differently than any other service he's tried.

**Revenue moment**: None yet. This is free. But Karim just went from "another contact form I filled out" to "these people understand my situation." When he receives his portal access two days later, he logs in immediately.

**What this demonstrates**: Re-New's process mastery creates trust before any human interaction. The personalized intelligence proves that the questionnaire isn't data collection for Re-New's benefit. It's the beginning of a structured relationship where the repreneur gets something back. Every competitor sends a generic confirmation. Re-New sends insight.

**What this demonstrates**: Making the invisible visible is its own value proposition. Sophie already had strengths and gaps. She just couldn't see them. #### Moment 3: Taking the "Evaluation Competent" Certification

Marc, 38, has been on the Pro plan for 4 months. He's sourced 6 targets, eliminated 4, and is seriously evaluating 2. He wants the "Evaluation Competent" badge because he's noticed it appears on his shareable profile.

He clicks "Start Assessment" on the certification page. Two options appear:

**Option A: Online assessment** (included in Pro). A 45-minute case study: he receives real (anonymized) financial statements from a French SME. He must identify 3 red flags, estimate a valuation range, and write a go/no-go recommendation. Re-New's system scores his analysis against expert answers.

**Option B: Supervised evaluation** (consulting session, 800 EUR). He brings a real target he's evaluating. A Re-New advisor walks through the analysis with him, corrects his approach, and certifies his competence based on the live exercise.

Marc chooses Option B because he has a real target and wants the coaching too. Two birds, one stone.

**What Marc feels**: "This isn't a quiz. I'm actually learning something I need right now, on my real deal." The certification isn't a hoop to jump through. It's the work itself, validated. And when he sees the badge appear on his profile, it's not a participation trophy. He earned it by demonstrating real analytical capability.

**Revenue moment**: 800 EUR consulting session + Marc's Pro subscription continues. The supervised evaluation also surfaces that Marc needs help structuring his LOI. He books a follow-up LDC Review session (1,000 EUR). One certification triggers 1,800 EUR in revenue.

**What this demonstrates**: Certifications generate revenue by being genuinely useful, not by being mandatory. Marc didn't need the badge. He wanted it because it was tied to real work on a real deal. The consulting upsell happens organically because the evaluation reveals the next gap. Re-New doesn't sell services. The journey surfaces needs, and the platform connects those needs to solutions.

**Revenue moment**: If this deal closes at 1.8M, Re-New earns 2% commission = 36,000 EUR. The repreneur's journey to this point already generated approximately 12,000 in subscription and consulting revenue over 10 months. Total lifetime value from this one repreneur: ~48,000 EUR.

**What this demonstrates**: Re-New's certification system closes the trust gap that kills deals. Sellers waste months with unqualified buyers. Intermediaries waste introductions on people who can't close. Re-New's readiness radar answers in one glance the question that normally takes three meetings: "Is this buyer real?" That's why sellers will prefer Re-New candidates, and why the commission model works. Re-New doesn't charge for matchmaking. Re-New charges because its preparation process produces buyers who actually close.

## 5. The Certification Architecture (Overview)

Instead of a single "certification level," Re-New shows a **readiness radar** across 6 dimensions, scored 1-9:

1. **Financial Capacity**: Proof of funds, financing structure, post-close liquidity
2. **Industry Expertise**: Years in sector, C-level roles, domain knowledge
3. **Deal Experience**: Prior acquisitions, advisory background, training completed
4. **Operational Capability**: Team management, crisis handling, change leadership
5. **Network Strength**: Advisors retained, board members, industry connections
6. **Process Maturity**: Milestones completed, certifications earned, timeline adherence

This maps directly onto Re-New's existing 3-tier assessment:
- **WHO score** feeds dimensions 2, 4 (experience, leadership)
- **WHEN score** feeds dimensions 1, 6 (financial structure, project maturity)
- **Tier 2 interview** feeds dimensions 2, 3, 4, 5 (competence dimensions)
- **Tier 3 milestones** feed dimension 6 and validate others

## 7. Why This Works

### For Bertrand and the Team

- **Leverages what's built**: The 3-tier assessment, dual scoring, journey stages, and milestone tracking are already in production. This strategy adds a repreneur-facing layer on top.
- **Keeps consulting core**: Coaching and consulting remain central. The platform amplifies reach, not replaces the human touch.
- **Multiple revenue streams**: Not dependent on any single source. Subscriptions, coaching, commissions, and AI credits diversify risk.
- **Gradual rollout**: Each phase delivers value independently. We don't need to build everything to start generating revenue.

### For Repreneurs

- **Clarity**: They know exactly where they are and what to do next
- **Progress**: Visible milestones and certifications create motivation
- **Credibility**: Certifications make them more attractive to sellers
- **Resources**: Structured content replaces scattered Google searches
- **Community**: They're not alone in the process

### For Sellers and Intermediaries

- **Trust**: Re-New certification means verified capability, not just expressed interest
- **Efficiency**: The readiness radar tells them instantly if a buyer is serious
- **Quality**: Pre-qualified, coached, certified buyers close faster
