## 1. The Market Opportunity

### The French SME Succession Crisis

**370,000 French SMEs** plan to transfer ownership by 2030 (BPI France study). At current rates, only 130,000 will successfully find buyers. **200,000 businesses risk failing to find a successor.** 3 million jobs are at stake.

The problem isn't supply. There are plenty of businesses to buy. The problem is **buyer readiness**:
- 70-75% of all M&A deals fail to create value (Fortune, 40,000 deals analyzed)
- **90% of first-time small business buyers fail** in their first attempt
- Average deal timeline: 12-18 months, most buyers underestimate by 6+ months

### Why Buyers Fail: 7 Systemic Readiness Gaps

| # | Gap | Impact |
|---|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Financial skills gap** | Can't evaluate deals or structure financing |
| 2 | **Negotiation inexperience** | Emotionally driven, not strategically |
| 3 | **Emotional decision-making** | "Falling in love" with a target, ignoring red flags |
| 4 | **Timeline underestimation** | Typical deal takes 12-18 months, not 3-6 |
| 5 | **Insufficient advisory team** | No lawyer, no accountant, no M&A advisor |
| 6 | **No integration plan** | Buy the business, then figure it out |
| 7 | **Isolation** | No peer network, no coaching during the process |

These gaps are **systemic and predictable**. They can be measured, addressed, and verified. That's what Re-New does.

### The Infrastructure Gap

The French acquisition ecosystem already has:
- **Deal sourcing platforms**: BPI Transmission (45,000+ listings), Transentreprise (6,000+), Fusacq (2,000+), CessionPME (118,000), Alvo
- **Training programs**: CRA (3-week flagship, 37 years of history), CCI ("5 jours pour reprendre"), ALTHEO
- **Government support**: BPI France (financing guarantees, matching, Pass Repreneur), CCI programs
- **Institutional CRMs**: DealCloud, Midaxo (designed for PE firms, not individual buyers)

What does **NOT** exist anywhere:
1. A comprehensive **repreneur journey management platform** from ideation to first 100 days
2. A **competence development dashboard** showing readiness evolution over time
3. A **certification system** that makes buyer readiness visible and verifiable to sellers
4. An **integrated platform** combining assessment, preparation, deal sourcing, and coaching

**This is Re-New's white space.**

## 2. Re-New's Strategic Position

### What Re-New Already Has (And Competitors Don't)

#### Process Mastery Encoded in Software

The 3-tier assessment system isn't just scoring. It's a structured framework that:
- Separates profile quality (WHO: 0-100) from project maturity (WHEN: 0-100)
- Uses financial triangulation to detect incoherence (ambition vs. means)
- Flags logical contradictions (5 warning flags that override scores)
- Maps 11 readiness milestones across 4 journey stages
- Provides automatic recommendations (deal_flow / priority_interview / interview / starter_pack)

No training program, no deal platform, no government agency does this.

#### The Dual Scoring Innovation

Traditional M&A qualification asks "is this buyer good?" Re-New asks two separate questions:
- **WHO**: Can this person execute? (experience, leadership, crisis management, investment decisions)
- **WHEN**: Is the project viable? (deal size vs. equity vs. structure coherence)

A brilliant operator with no financial plan gets different guidance than a well-funded novice. This nuance is Re-New's intellectual property.

#### The Journey-Centric Data Model

The platform already tracks repreneurs across:
- **Lifecycle stages**: lead / qualified / client
- **Journey stages**: explorer / learner / ready / serial_acquirer
- **11 binary milestones** across 4 journey categories

This is the foundation for a certification architecture, not just a CRM.

### What Re-New Doesn't Have Yet

- No repreneur-facing portal (repreneurs can't see their own data)
- No content library (knowledge is in Bertrand's head)
- No payment processing
- No certification display or verification system
- No AI-powered tools for repreneurs
- No seller/intermediary integration

## 3. The Platform Vision

### Concept: Curated Acceleration Platform

Re-New becomes the **quality assurance layer** in the French acquisition market.

**For repreneurs**: "Re-New is where I go to become a serious, certified buyer. The platform shows me exactly where I am in my journey, what I need to learn next, what milestones I'm missing, and connects me with the right resources and deals when I'm ready."

**For sellers/intermediaries** (V2.0+, manual at first): "When I see a Re-New certified repreneur, I know they've been vetted, scored, coached, and verified. I trust them because Re-New's certification means something concrete: validated financing, documented industry expertise, completed preparation."

**For Re-New**: "We own the buyer side of the French SME acquisition market. Every repreneur in our platform is tracked, scored, and guided. We monetize through subscriptions, coaching, and deal commissions."

### How It Works

```
1. INTAKE (public)
   Repreneur fills questionnaire
   -> automatic WHO/WHEN scoring + warning flags

2. GATE (team decision)
   Minimum score threshold
   -> accepted or redirected to resources

3. ONBOARDING (the moment of value)
   Personalized intelligence message showing Re-New "gets it"
   -> "Based on your profile, you're at [stage]. Your strengths: [X,Y].
      Your gaps: [A,B]. Here's what to focus on next."

4. PORTAL (repreneur-facing)
   - Journey map showing position and progress
   - Next recommended actions
   - Competence radar chart (6-dimension readiness)
   - Milestone tracking with certification badges
   - Resource library (gated by tier)
   - AI tools (gated by credits/tier)

5. DEVELOPMENT (ongoing)
   - Self-paced resources (free tier)
   - Coaching sessions (paid)
   - Certification exams/validations (automated + human)
   - Community/peer network

6. DEAL FLOW (for qualified repreneurs)
   - Access to acquisition opportunities
   - AI-powered matching based on profile + preferences
   - Re-New facilitation + commission on closed deals
```

## 4. The 13-Phase Acquisition Lifecycle

Based on research across ETA frameworks (Stanford, IESE, HBS), French market specifics (CRA, CCI, BPI), and M&A best practices, here is the full buyer's journey mapped as a scaffold that Re-New populates with resources, tools, and certifications.

### Phase 0: Ideation & Exploration
*"Should I buy a business?" (1-3 months)*

The repreneur explores whether acquisition is the right path. **Deliverable**: personal decision to commit. **Certification**: Explorer Badge (auto-granted on questionnaire completion). **Key resources to create**: "Is Repreneurship For You?" self-assessment guide, FAQ on acquisition vs. startup. **Platform tools**: intake questionnaire (EXISTS), personalized onboarding message (TO BUILD).

### Phase 1: Self-Assessment & Project Definition
*"What kind of buyer am I?" (1-2 months)*

The repreneur moves from curiosity to self-examination, articulating their personal acquisition thesis. **Deliverable**: written "Projet de Reprise" document. **Certification**: Profile Verified (Re-New team validates WHO score after interview). **Key resources to create**: "Projet de Reprise" template, buyer type guide (4 personas). **Platform tools**: WHO score radar chart visible to repreneur, gap analysis generator.

### Phase 2: Building Acquisition Thesis
*"What am I looking for?" (1-2 months)*

The repreneur defines target criteria: sectors, geography, deal size, deal-breakers. **Deliverable**: Target Screening Scorecard. **Certification**: Thesis Validated (Re-New reviews and confirms criteria are realistic). **Key resources to create**: sector analysis templates, "How to Define Your Target Profile" guide. **Platform tools**: target profile builder, sector comparison tool.

### Phase 3: Financial Preparation
*"Can I actually afford this?" (2-3 months)*

The most critical preparation phase. Understanding financial capacity, the French financing stack, and getting bank commitments. **Deliverable**: financial capacity assessment + bank pre-approval. **Certification**: Financially Verified (proof of funds or pre-approval letter uploaded, verified by Re-New). **Key resources to create**: "French Acquisition Financing 101", LBO structure explainer, BPI France programs guide. **Platform tools**: financial capacity calculator, deal size simulator.

### Phase 4: Advisory Team Assembly
*"Who's on my team?" (1-2 months)*

No successful acquisition happens alone. Minimum: lawyer (M&A), accountant (DD), M&A advisor. **Deliverable**: retained advisory team documented. **Certification**: Team Ready (team composition verified by Re-New). **Key resources to create**: "Building Your Dream Team" guide, how to evaluate an M&A advisor. **Platform tools**: advisory team tracker.

### Phase 5: Deal Sourcing
*"Where do I find businesses to buy?" (3-6 months, ongoing)*

Active search across deal platforms (BPI Transmission, Transentreprise, Fusacq, CessionPME), intermediaries, and direct approaches. **Deliverable**: active deal pipeline with multiple targets. **Certification**: Active Searcher (Re-New confirms active pipeline for 30+ days). **Key resources to create**: "French Deal Sourcing Channels" guide, how to read an Information Memorandum. **Platform tools**: deal pipeline tracker, AI-powered deal matching.

### Phase 6: Target Evaluation & Screening
*"Is this specific business worth pursuing?" (2-4 weeks per target)*

First real analytical test: reading financials, normalizing EBITDA, estimating valuation, making go/no-go decisions. **Deliverable**: go/no-go decision with initial valuation range. **Certification**: Evaluation Competent (passed assessment or demonstrated through supervised practice). **Key resources to create**: "How to Read French Financial Statements" guide, valuation methods comparison, red flags checklist. **Platform tools**: AI business evaluator, valuation calculator.

### Phase 7: Letter of Intent (LOI) / Lettre de Cadrage
*"Making it official" (2-4 weeks)*

Formal offer with proposed terms, price range, conditions, and exclusivity request. **Deliverable**: signed LOI/LDC with exclusivity. **Certification**: LDC Validated (Re-New reviews LDC before submission; already exists as milestone). **Key resources to create**: LOI/LDC template, key clauses explainer, price justification methodology. **Platform tools**: LDC builder with AI assistance.

### Phase 8: Due Diligence
*"What am I really buying?" (45-60 days)*

Comprehensive DD across 6 workstreams: financial, legal, tax, operational, HR, environmental. Where deals die or get restructured. **Deliverable**: DD report with risk register. **Certification**: DD Competent (completed preparation course + assessment, or supervised DD). **Key resources to create**: DD checklist by workstream, "What to Look For" guides, common red flags. **Platform tools**: DD checklist tracker, document vault, AI DD question generator.

### Phase 9: Negotiation & Deal Structuring
*"Getting to the right price and terms" (2-4 weeks)*

Price adjustment based on DD findings, GAP structuring, earn-out mechanics, seller financing. **Deliverable**: draft protocole de cession / SPA. **Certification**: Negotiation Ready (completed workshop or demonstrated through deal history). **Key resources to create**: negotiation tactics guide, GAP structure explainer, SPA walkthrough. **Platform tools**: deal term sheet builder, GAP calculator.

### Phase 10: Financing Finalization
*"Getting the money" (2-4 weeks)*

Binding commitments from lenders, holding company setup, BPI guarantee applications. **Deliverable**: binding commitment letters + holding structure finalized. **Certification**: Financing Secured (verified commitment letters). **Key resources to create**: bank presentation template, financial model templates, holding company setup guide. **Platform tools**: financial model builder, bank submission tracker.

### Phase 11: Closing
*"Signing day" (1-2 weeks)*

Legal ownership transfer. Administrative but legally critical. **Deliverable**: ownership transferred, funds transferred. **Certification**: Deal Closed (proof of closing verified). **Key resources to create**: closing day checklist, post-signing administrative guide, day-one communication templates. **Platform tools**: closing countdown tracker.

### Phase 12: Transition & Handover
*"Taking the wheel" (3-12 months)*

Seller hands over operational control. Building trust with the team while implementing your vision. **Deliverable**: operational independence established. **Certification**: Transition Complete (Re-New confirms independence). **Key resources to create**: "First Meeting with Your New Team" guide, knowledge transfer checklist, 30-60-90 day plan template. **Platform tools**: transition task tracker.

### Phase 13: First 100 Days
*"Making it yours" (~3 months)*

The critical period. 70-90% of M&A value destruction happens during integration. **Deliverable**: operations stabilized, quick wins achieved, credibility established. **Certification**: Certified Repreneur (Re-New's highest individual certification, full journey completed). **Key resources to create**: "First 100 Days Playbook", quick wins framework, team assessment guide. **Platform tools**: first 100 days tracker, performance dashboard.

| Phase | Certification | Verification | Access Tier |
|-------|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| 0 Ideation | Explorer | Auto (questionnaire done) | Free |
| 1 Self-Assessment | Profile Verified | Human (Re-New interview) | Free |
| 2 Thesis | Thesis Validated | Human (Re-New review) | Coaching |

| 4 Team | Team Ready | Self-declared + verified | Pro |

| 8 Due Diligence | DD Competent | Course + assessment | Consulting |
| 9 Negotiation | Negotiation Ready | Workshop or experience | Consulting |

## 5. The Certification Architecture

### Design Principles

Certifications are credible when they are **hard to get and easy to verify** (CFA, PMP, ICF models). Each Re-New certification answers a specific question a seller or intermediary would ask: "Can this buyer successfully close and run my business?"

Design rules:
- **Verified, not self-declared**: every certification requires evidence or automated verification
- **Achievement-based, not gamification**: badges represent real milestones
- **Useful to third parties**: each answers a question sellers actually ask
- **Progressive**: later certifications build on earlier ones
- **Revenue-aligned**: higher certifications require engagement that generates revenue

### The 6-Dimension Readiness Radar

Instead of a single score, Re-New displays a multi-dimensional readiness profile:

| Dimension | What It Measures | Data Sources |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Financial Capacity** (1-9) | Proof of funds, financing structure, post-close liquidity | WHEN score, Financially Verified cert, Financing Secured cert |
| **Industry Expertise** (1-9) | Years in target sectors, C-level experience, domain knowledge | WHO score, Tier 2 interview, Evaluation Competent cert |
| **Deal Experience** (1-9) | Prior acquisitions, advisory background, training completed | WHO score (M&A exposure), Tier 2 interview, deal certifications |
| **Operational Capability** (1-9) | Team management, crisis handling, change leadership | WHO score (leadership), Tier 2 interview |
| **Network Strength** (1-9) | Advisors retained, board, industry connections | Team Ready cert, Tier 2 interview |
| **Process Maturity** (1-9) | Milestones completed, certifications earned, timeline adherence | Tier 3 milestones, certification count |

Each dimension uses a 1-9 scale inspired by Technology Readiness Level (TRL) frameworks: 1 = Awareness, 3 = Foundation, 5 = Competent, 7 = Advanced, 9 = Mastery (proven through completed journey).

### How Existing Data Maps to the Radar

The radar is not a new assessment. It's a new **visualization** of data Re-New already collects:

- **WHO score** (automated) feeds Industry Expertise, Operational Capability, Deal Experience
- **WHEN score** (automated) feeds Financial Capacity, Process Maturity
- **Tier 2 interview** (human) feeds all competence dimensions
- **Tier 3 milestones** feed Process Maturity and validate other dimensions

### What Sellers See

When Re-New shares a repreneur profile with a seller or intermediary:

```
REPRENEUR PROFILE: [Name]
Re-New Certified: [Stage] | Journey Phase: [X/13]

Readiness Radar:
  Financial Capacity:     ======== 8/9  (Financing secured: bank + BPI)
  Industry Expertise:     ======   6/9  (12 years manufacturing, COO level)
  Deal Experience:        ===      3/9  (First acquisition, CRA training done)
  Operational Capability: ======== 8/9  (50+ person teams, crisis managed)
  Network Strength:       =====    5/9  (Lawyer + accountant, no M&A advisor)
  Process Maturity:       =======  7/9  (9/13 certifications, LDC validated)

Target: Manufacturing | 1-3M EUR | Rhone-Alpes
Financial: 400K equity + bank pre-approval for 1.2M
Certifications: Explorer, Profile Verified, Thesis Validated,
                Financially Verified, Team Ready, Active Searcher,
                LDC Validated, DD Competent, Negotiation Ready
```

This is infinitely more useful to a seller than "our candidate is interested in your business."

### Certification Progression

Prerequisites ensure credibility. Not all certifications are available immediately:

- **Explorer** (no prerequisite) -> **Profile Verified** (requires Explorer)
- After Profile Verified, three tracks can run in parallel: **Thesis Validated**, **Financially Verified**, **Team Ready**
- **Active Searcher** requires both Thesis Validated AND Financially Verified (know what you want AND can pay for it)
- From Active Searcher onward, certifications are sequential through the deal process
- Some certifications expire: Financially Verified every 6 months (re-upload proof), Thesis Validated annually (update criteria), Active Searcher after 3 months inactive
- Deal-specific certifications (LDC Validated through Certified Repreneur) never expire

## 6. Revenue Model

Five revenue streams, phased over 12 months. Specific pricing TBD pending market validation with initial cohort.

### Stream 1: Platform Subscriptions

| Tier | Indicative Price | Access |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| **Free (Bronze)** | 0 | Journey map, basic resources, Explorer badge, community read-only |
| **Pro (Silver)** | ~99-149/month | Full resources, AI credits, tools, milestone tracking, 5+ certifications |
| **Premium (Gold)** | ~299-499/month | Dedicated advisor, priority deal flow, all AI tools, seller visibility |

Conversion logic: Free shows the repreneur what they need. Pro gives them tools to act. Premium adds human support and deal access.

### Stream 2: Coaching & Consulting

Existing revenue stream, amplified by platform. The journey surfaces consulting needs at the right moment.

| Service | Indicative Range | When |
|---------|-----------------|------|
| Starter Pack (structured program) | 2,000-5,000 | Explorer/Learner stage |
| Sparring Partner (ongoing) | 200-500/session | Active search phase |
| Target/Deal Review | 500-1,500 | Evaluation + LOI phase |
| DD/Negotiation Accompaniment | 1,000-5,000 | Deal execution phase |

### Stream 3: Deal Commissions

The primary long-term revenue driver. 1-3% of deal value on facilitated acquisitions.

Indicative: a 2M deal at 2% = 40,000 EUR. Even 3-5 deals/year generates 120-200K before subscriptions or coaching.

### Stream 4: Certification Fees (TBD)

Assessment and course fees for specific certifications (Evaluation Competent, DD Competent, Negotiation Ready). Indicative: 300-800 EUR per certification.

### Stream 5: AI Credits (TBD)

Pay-per-use AI tools (business valuation analysis, DD question generator, deal structure optimizer). Credits included in Pro/Premium subscriptions, additional credits purchasable.

### Revenue Trajectory

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|--|--------|--------|--------|
| **Conservative** | ~250K | ~700K | ~1.6M |
| **Base** | ~300K | ~900K | ~2.0M |
| **Optimistic** | ~600K | ~1.8M | ~4.5M |

Year 1 is consulting-led (existing capability). By Year 3, commissions and subscriptions should dominate, indicating the platform creates independent value beyond digitized consulting.

**Estimated lifetime value per completed repreneur journey**: ~48,000 EUR (subscriptions + consulting + deal commission).
