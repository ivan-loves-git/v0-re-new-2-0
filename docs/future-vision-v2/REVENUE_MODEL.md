## Revenue Design Philosophy

Re-New's revenue model follows one principle: **revenue should be proportional to value delivered**.

- Exploration is free (because the value is in expanding the funnel)
- Preparation requires a subscription (because structured guidance has measurable value)
- Deal execution generates commissions (because a closed deal is the ultimate proof of value)
- AI tools are credits-based (because usage tracks value consumed)

This hybrid model avoids the two extremes:
- Pure SaaS (subscription only): risks charging repreneurs who aren't ready to pay
- Pure commission: creates incentive to push deals rather than prepare buyers properly

## Revenue Stream 3: Deal Commissions

### Commission Structure

| Deal Size | Commission Rate | Commission Amount |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Under 500K | 3% | Up to 15,000 |
| 500K - 1M | 2.5% | 12,500 - 25,000 |
| 1M - 2M | 2% | 20,000 - 40,000 |
| 2M - 5M | 1.5% | 30,000 - 75,000 |
| Over 5M | 1% (negotiable) | 50,000+ |

Alternative: **Lehman formula** (sliding scale)
- 5% of first 1M + 4% of next 1M + 3% of next 1M + 2% of next 1M + 1% above 4M

### When Commissions Apply

Commissions are earned when Re-New facilitates the deal:
- Re-New introduced the buyer to the opportunity (matchmaking)
- Re-New provided consulting support through the deal process
- Re-New's certification process contributed to deal credibility

Commission is NOT earned for:
- Deals the repreneur found entirely independently
- Deals where Re-New only provided platform access (subscription covers that)

**Key insight**: Deal commissions are the primary revenue driver long-term. Even modest deal volume generates significant revenue. The platform's role is to (a) increase deal volume by better preparing buyers and (b) capture commission by being integral to the deal process.

## Key Assumptions and Risks

### Assumptions

1. **Market size**: 30,000 active acquisition searchers per year in France (BPI France data)
2. **Platform reach**: 0.5-2% market penetration by Year 3
3. **Conversion rates**: 10-20% Free to Pro, 2-6% Free to Premium
4. **Deal close rate**: 20% of repreneurs in deal pipeline close within 12 months
5. **Average deal size**: 1.5-2.0M (typical French SME acquisition)
6. **Consulting pricing**: In line with CRA and independent M&A consultants
7. **Churn**: 15-20% annual for Pro, 10-15% for Premium (lower due to higher engagement)

### Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Low conversion Free to Pro | Subscription revenue below projections | Focus on demonstrating value through free tier; optimize onboarding message |
| Few deals closed | Commission revenue absent | Consulting revenue provides base; don't depend on commissions in Year 1 |
| Content creation bottleneck | Resource library stays empty, no reason to upgrade | Start with 5 foundational guides; iterate based on demand |
| Competitor launches similar | Market share pressure | First-mover advantage with existing CRM + 3-tier assessment; execution speed |
| Regulatory changes (M&A advisory) | May require licensing for certain services | Structure commissions as "facilitation fees"; consult legal counsel |
| AI costs higher than projected | Margin compression on AI credits | Credit pricing gives flexibility; adjust pricing with costs |

## Phased Revenue Activation

### Month 1-2 (Phase A: Foundation)
**Revenue**: Consulting only (existing)
- Platform is free (repreneur portal launches)
- Value demonstration through onboarding message
- Begin measuring engagement metrics

### Month 3-4 (Phase B: Engagement)
**Revenue**: Consulting + first subscriptions
- Pro subscription launches (target: 10-20 subscribers)
- Resource library justifies subscription
- Payment integration (Stripe)

### Month 5-6 (Phase C: Intelligence)
**Revenue**: Consulting + subscriptions + AI credits
- AI tools launch (credits system)
- Coaching booking integrated
- Deal matching begins

### Month 7-9 (Phase D: Certification)
**Revenue**: All streams except marketplace commissions
- Certification exams available
- Assessment fees active
- Certification drives consulting demand

### Month 10-12 (Phase E: Marketplace)
**Revenue**: All 5 streams active
- Deal commissions from facilitated introductions
- Seller/intermediary access creates network effects
- Full revenue model operational

## Decision Points

The following decisions need alignment with Bertrand before implementation:

1. **Subscription pricing**: 99 vs. 149 for Pro, 299 vs. 499 for Premium (test sensitivity)
2. **Commission structure**: Fixed percentage vs. Lehman formula
3. **Commission triggers**: What qualifies as "Re-New facilitated" vs. independent
4. **AI credit pricing**: 0.20/credit or adjust based on actual API costs
5. **Consulting pricing**: Current rates vs. proposed rates (any adjustment needed?)
6. **Payment timing**: Monthly billing vs. annual discount (10-15% for annual?)
7. **Free tier limits**: How much to give away (balance lead gen vs. conversion pressure)
