# Readiness You Can Prove

### A vision for what Re-New could become

---

What does every aspiring buyer in France hear when they decide to acquire a business?

"Good luck."

That's it. Good luck navigating the financing. Good luck finding the right target. Good luck convincing a seller who spent thirty years building something that you, a complete stranger, deserve to carry it forward.

They get a handshake. Maybe a seminar. Maybe a broker who returns their calls. And then they're alone, navigating the most complex financial decision of their lives with no map, no milestones, and no way to prove they're ready.

This is the gap Re-New sits in. And what makes it unusual is that Re-New is the only vertical player operating in this space. Not a generalist platform that added an M&A tab. Not a training program that bolted on deal flow. For two years, bright professionals have done nothing but speak to repreneurs, listen to sellers, sit with intermediaries, and map every challenge, every fear, every failure mode in this market. That kind of focused immersion produces something you can't buy or copy: pattern recognition encoded in a system.

And that system already exists.

---

The platform is live. It works. It replaced the old ATS. The team can manage repreneurs, score their readiness across multiple dimensions, track their journey through the acquisition lifecycle, and send personalized communications. In about eight weeks, it went from zero to a functioning intelligence layer.

But here's the thing worth noticing: what sits behind the admin dashboard is genuinely sophisticated. A three-tier assessment that separates who someone is from whether their project makes sense. A dual scoring model that treats a brilliant operator with no financial plan completely differently from a well-funded novice. Financial triangulation that catches incoherence before it becomes a problem. Eleven readiness milestones across four journey stages. Automatic recommendations based on profile patterns.

No training program does this. No deal platform does this. No government agency does this. This isn't just a CRM. It's a methodology that became software.

The problem is, the people who matter most can't see any of it. Repreneurs fill a questionnaire. They get a thank you email. And then they wait. They have no visibility into their own scores, their journey stage, or what they should focus on next. The intelligence exists. It's just facing the wrong direction.

---

The suggestion is simple: turn it around.

Take what already exists behind the dashboard and make it visible to the repreneurs themselves. Let the methodology become the product. Let the structured journey, the scoring, the milestones, the readiness dimensions become something people can see, interact with, and use to actually get better at buying a business.

The shift, in one sentence: from internal CRM to repreneur-facing acceleration platform.

To make this concrete, picture four moments.

---

**The onboarding message.** Karim, 45, operations director in logistics. He submits the intake questionnaire. He expects a generic confirmation. Instead, twenty minutes later, he receives a message that references his 18 years of leadership experience, notes his target of industrial services in the 1-2M range, and flags that his equity target of 150K against a 1.5M deal suggests a financing ratio most banks will question. The message tells him he's in the Explorer phase and gives him three specific things to focus on next.

What Karim feels is recognition. Someone actually read his answers. Someone spotted the financing gap he was already worried about. Someone knows what they're doing. Every competitor sends a generic "thanks, we'll be in touch." Re-New sends insight. The questionnaire stops being data collection for the company's benefit and becomes the beginning of a relationship where the repreneur gets something back. No revenue yet. But Karim went from "another form" to "these people understand my situation" in twenty minutes. When he gets portal access, he logs in immediately.

---

**First login.** Sophie, 42, ex-COO of a manufacturing company. She's had her Re-New interview and just received portal access. She logs in and sees her readiness radar: six dimensions scored from 1 to 9. Operational Capability at 9. Industry Expertise at 8. But Deal Experience at 2 and Process Maturity at 2. Next to each gap, specific guidance on what to do to move the score up. Below the radar, a journey map showing 13 phases with two green badges earned and the rest visible but grey. A clear path forward.

For the first time, someone gave Sophie a map instead of telling her to just start looking. She can see her strengths. She can see her gaps. She knows what to work on. She clicks on a tool called "Build Target Screening Scorecard" and hits the Pro paywall. She subscribes. Three minutes on the platform. Not because anyone sold her anything. Because the map was too good to walk away from.

---

**Earning a certification.** Marc, 38, Pro subscriber for four months. He's evaluating two targets and wants the "Evaluation Competent" certification for his profile. He has two options: an online assessment included in his subscription, or a supervised evaluation session on his real target with a Re-New advisor. He picks the real target. Real coaching, real deal, real certification. The session also reveals he needs help structuring his Letter of Intent, so he books a follow-up. One certification triggers 1,800 EUR in consulting revenue.

The point is that certifications generate revenue by being genuinely useful, not by being mandatory. The journey surfaces needs. The platform connects those needs to solutions at the exact moment the repreneur is most motivated. Re-New doesn't need to sell services. It surfaces the right service at the right moment.

---

**The seller's perspective.** Jean-Pierre, 61, owns a plumbing services company. His intermediary presents two candidates.

Candidate A, from another platform: "Interested buyer, 15 years business experience, has financing." One paragraph.

Candidate B, Re-New certified: a readiness radar showing Financial Capacity 8/9 with bank pre-approval verified, Industry Expertise 6/9 with twelve years in services, Operational Capability 8/9, Process Maturity 7/9 with nine certifications earned and an LOI validated on a previous target. Plus documentation of completed due diligence training and negotiation preparation.

Jean-Pierre asks to meet Candidate B first. Of course he does. The readiness radar answers in one glance the question that normally takes three meetings and months of uncertainty: is this buyer real?

The deal closes at 1.8M. Re-New earns a commission. The repreneur's journey already generated subscription and consulting revenue over ten months. Total lifetime value from a single repreneur: close to 50,000 EUR.

---

Behind these four moments sits a structured framework. A 13-phase acquisition lifecycle from Ideation to First 100 Days. 14 certifications, each verified and each answering a question a seller would actually ask. A six-dimension readiness radar that reorganizes data the platform already collects into a format useful to both repreneurs and sellers. Five revenue streams that build on each other: subscriptions, coaching, certifications, deal commissions, and AI-powered tools.

None of this requires starting over. The scoring engine exists. The journey tracking exists. The milestone system exists. The intelligence is already there. What would be new is a repreneur-facing layer that makes it visible, interactive, and valuable to the people going through the process.

---

The reason this could work comes down to three things.

For repreneurs, it creates clarity. They know where they stand, what to do next, and how to prove they're ready. For the first time, someone gives them a structured path through the most disorienting process of their professional lives.

For sellers and intermediaries, it creates trust. A Re-New certification would mean verified capability, not just expressed interest. The readiness radar tells them instantly whether a buyer is serious. Pre-qualified, coached, certified buyers close faster and waste less of everyone's time.

For Re-New, it leverages everything that's already built. The platform amplifies the team's reach without replacing the human touch. Multiple revenue streams reduce dependence on any single source. And each piece delivers value independently, so nothing needs to happen all at once.

---

This isn't about technology. It isn't about features. It's about a single idea.

Every aspiring buyer in France is invisible. Their readiness is invisible. Their preparation is invisible. Their seriousness is invisible. And because it's invisible, sellers don't trust them, banks hesitate, intermediaries hedge, and deals die.

Re-New is in a position to change that. Not by adding more tools to a crowded market, but by making something visible that was always there but nobody could see.

Readiness you can prove.

That's the promise to repreneurs: we will make you provably ready. And that's the promise to sellers: our buyers are provably real.

Not everything needs to happen. And not everything needs to happen at once. But the foundation is there, the market gap is clear, and the first step is the same one Re-New has always taken: listen, understand, and build something that actually helps.
