Executive Summary

The material exposure is closed. Full sign-off is not yet.

The audit validated 23 reportable security findings. Fourteen are now closed in production, including every original high-risk finding. The work protects the core CRM dataset, staff roles, exports, opportunities, documents and email operations from unauthorized access or action.

WAVE can continue controlled staff operations and monitored repreneur use. Nine findings remain before full security sign-off. Five are release gates around identity claiming, unsafe public content, public uploads and assessment access. The remaining four deepen login protection and staff-only control over offers and imports. One separate test credential check is still deferred.

Caveats and assumptions

What this report does and does not prove

The status is evidence-backed, but security is a maintained operating condition rather than a one-time certificate.

  • Scope: the audit covered all 58 defined repository areas at the reviewed revision and used bounded, non-destructive production checks.
  • Closure: closed means the control was changed and verified through tests and proportionate production evidence. It does not mean every possible future attack path has been eliminated.
  • Deferred credential: its presence was proven, but validity was intentionally not tested. Removal and rotation are the safe completion action.
  • Incident status: the audit proves vulnerable paths and current controls. It does not prove whether a past incident did or did not occur.
  • Historical scope: repository-history sanitization is excluded from this report. The closure count reflects the current deployed product and tracked files.
