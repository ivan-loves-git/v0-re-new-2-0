Executive Summary

The audited security foundation is closed and verified.

The audit produced 23 reportable findings and one deferred credential-hygiene candidate. All 24 are now resolved. The work protects the core CRM dataset, staff roles, exports, opportunities, documents, email operations, identity flows, public content and uploads from the validated attack paths.

Production proof covered four owned account states, blocked public signup, database isolation, staff-only workflows, sanitized or isolated stored content, one-use upload authority and live login throttling. There is no meaningful audit remediation backlog. What remains is continuous security maintenance as WAVE grows, not unfinished foundation work.

What’s next for cyber

Security is now part of our normal operating rhythm.

All risks found through the independent audits have been fixed and rechecked. We will keep it that way with a simple routine: a security review every week, a security sign-off before every major release, and one full independent audit every month. Anything new that is found will be tracked and fixed to closure.
