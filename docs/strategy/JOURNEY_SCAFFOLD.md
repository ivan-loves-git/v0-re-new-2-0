# Re-New Journey Scaffold: The 13-Phase Acquisition Lifecycle

**Reference document for the Re-New team**
*Companion to: [PLATFORM_VISION_V2.md](./PLATFORM_VISION_V2.md)*

---

## How to Read This Document

Each phase represents a distinct stage in the repreneur's acquisition journey. For each phase we define:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Description** | What the repreneur is doing at this stage |
| **Deliverable** | What they produce (tangible output) |
| **Competences** | Skills they need to develop or demonstrate |
| **Certification** | Re-New badge earned upon completion |
| **Resources** | Content Re-New needs to create |
| **Tools** | Platform features to build |
| **Timeline** | Typical duration |
| **Status** | Current build state (empty / planned / built) |

## Phase 0: Ideation & Exploration

**"Should I buy a business?"**

The repreneur is exploring whether acquisition is the right path. They may have just lost a job, be frustrated with corporate life, or be curious about entrepreneurship through acquisition. This is the discovery phase.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Personal decision: commit to exploring acquisition as a career path |
| **Competences** | Self-awareness, career assessment, risk tolerance evaluation, understanding of what "buying a business" actually means |
| **Certification** | **Explorer Badge** (auto-granted upon completing intake questionnaire) |
| **Resources to create** | "Is Repreneurship For You?" self-assessment guide; FAQ: acquisition vs. starting from scratch; Video concept: "A Day in the Life of a Repreneur"; Overview of the French acquisition market landscape |
| **Tools** | Intake questionnaire (EXISTS), personalized onboarding message (TO BUILD), journey map entry point |
| **Timeline** | 1-3 months |
| **Status** | Intake: BUILT. Onboarding message: PLANNED. Resources: EMPTY |

## Phase 1: Self-Assessment & Project Definition

**"What kind of buyer am I?"**

The repreneur moves from curiosity to self-examination. They assess their strengths, gaps, buyer type, and begin to articulate their personal acquisition thesis. Re-New's WHO score becomes a mirror.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Written "Projet de Reprise" document (personal acquisition thesis describing who they are as a buyer, what they bring, what they need) |
| **Competences** | Honest self-evaluation; understanding of buyer types (first-time, serial, corporate spinoff, family succession); ability to articulate strengths and gaps |
| **Certification** | **Profile Verified** (Re-New team validates WHO score after interview, confirms repreneur self-assessment is realistic) |
| **Resources to create** | "Projet de Reprise" template (fillable document); Buyer type guide (4 personas explained with examples); Strengths/gaps analysis framework; "What Sellers Really Look For in a Buyer" guide |
| **Tools** | WHO score radar chart (visible to repreneur in portal); Gap analysis generator (compares self-assessment to Re-New's assessment); "Projet de Reprise" builder (guided form) |
| **Timeline** | 1-2 months |
| **Status** | WHO scoring: BUILT. Portal display: PLANNED. Resources: EMPTY |

## Phase 2: Building Acquisition Thesis

**"What am I looking for?"**

The repreneur transitions from "who am I" to "what do I want." They define target criteria: sectors, geography, deal size, deal-breakers. This becomes their search compass.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Target Screening Scorecard (documented criteria: sectors, size range, geography, deal-breakers, must-haves) |
| **Competences** | Market understanding; sector analysis; competitive landscape awareness; realistic expectation-setting (what's available vs. what's desired) |
| **Certification** | **Thesis Validated** (Re-New reviews and confirms acquisition criteria are realistic and coherent) |
| **Resources to create** | Sector analysis templates (by industry vertical); "How to Define Your Target Profile" guide; Geographic market data by French region; Example target scorecards from past client journeys (anonymized); "Common Mistakes in Target Definition" |
| **Tools** | Target profile builder (guided form); Sector comparison tool; Market data dashboard (deal volume by sector/region) |
| **Timeline** | 1-2 months |
| **Status** | EMPTY (questionnaire captures some criteria but no dedicated tools) |

---

## Phase 3: Financial Preparation

**"Can I actually afford this?"**

The single most critical preparation phase. The repreneur must understand their financial capacity, the French financing stack, and get concrete commitments from banks. This is where financial triangulation from the WHEN score becomes actionable.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Financial capacity assessment completed; maximum deal size defined; bank pre-approval obtained |
| **Competences** | Understanding equity vs. debt structures; LBO mechanics; holding company setup (SAS, SARL); French financing stack (apport personnel, emprunt bancaire, love money, BPI guarantees, seller financing); post-acquisition cash flow management |
| **Certification** | **Financially Verified** (repreneur uploads proof of funds or bank pre-approval letter, verified by Re-New team) |
| **Resources to create** | "French Acquisition Financing 101" comprehensive guide; LBO structure explainer with examples; Equity calculator (basic version: free; advanced version: Pro); Bank pre-approval checklist (what banks want to see); BPI France programs guide (guarantees, co-financing, conditions); "How Much Can I Afford?" decision framework |
| **Tools** | Financial capacity calculator; Deal size simulator (AI-powered for Pro tier); Document upload + verification workflow; Financing structure comparison tool |
| **Timeline** | 2-3 months |
| **Status** | WHEN score captures financial data: BUILT. Calculators and verification: PLANNED |

## Phase 4: Advisory Team Assembly

**"Who's on my team?"**

No successful acquisition happens alone. The repreneur needs at minimum: a lawyer (specializing in M&A), an accountant (for due diligence), and ideally an M&A advisor/broker. This phase is about building the right team.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Retained lawyer, accountant, and M&A advisor (minimum); documented team with roles and engagement terms |
| **Competences** | Understanding advisory roles (who does what, when); evaluating professional quality (red flags, good signs); managing professional relationships and expectations; budgeting for advisory fees |
| **Certification** | **Team Ready** (advisory team confirmed and documented in platform; Re-New verifies team composition) |
| **Resources to create** | "Building Your Dream Team" guide (what each advisor does, when to engage them); How to evaluate an M&A advisor (10 questions to ask); Red flags in advisory relationships; Fee structures guide (fixed, hourly, success-based); Partner directory: Re-New vetted professionals (future) |
| **Tools** | Advisory team tracker (who's confirmed, who's pending, contact details); Team readiness checklist; Professional recommendation engine (future: match with vetted advisors) |
| **Timeline** | 1-2 months |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

---

## Phase 5: Deal Sourcing

**"Where do I find businesses to buy?"**

The active search begins. The repreneur engages with deal platforms (BPI Transmission, Transentreprise, Fusacq, CessionPME), intermediaries, and potentially direct approaches to business owners. Pipeline management becomes critical.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Active deal pipeline with multiple targets being tracked |
| **Competences** | Networking with intermediaries; reading Information Memoranda (IM); initial target screening (quick go/no-go); pipeline management discipline; direct approach techniques (contacting business owners) |
| **Certification** | **Active Searcher** (Re-New confirms active pipeline with consistent search activity over 30+ days) |
| **Resources to create** | "French Deal Sourcing Channels" comprehensive guide (BPI, Transentreprise, intermediaries, direct approach); How to read an Information Memorandum; How to approach business owners directly; Intermediary network map (who to contact by region/sector); "Pipeline Management for Repreneurs" (tracking targets effectively) |
| **Tools** | Deal pipeline tracker (targets with status, notes, next actions); AI-powered deal matching (based on target profile from Phase 2); Saved searches with alerts; Deal source comparison (which platforms for which deal types) |
| **Timeline** | 3-6 months (ongoing, overlaps with phases 6-11) |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

---

## Phase 6: Target Evaluation & Screening

**"Is this specific business worth pursuing?"**

For each target in the pipeline, the repreneur conducts initial evaluation. This is the first real analytical test: can they read financial statements, normalize EBITDA, estimate valuation, and make a go/no-go decision?

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Go/No-Go decision per target; initial valuation range; key risk identification |
| **Competences** | Financial statement analysis (bilan + compte de resultat); EBITDA normalization (removing owner perks, one-time items); Valuation methods (multiples, DCF, asset-based); Competitive positioning assessment; Key person/supplier dependency analysis |
| **Certification** | **Evaluation Competent** (passed Re-New evaluation assessment or demonstrated competence through supervised practice) |
| **Resources to create** | "How to Read French Financial Statements" guide; EBITDA normalization worksheet; Valuation methods comparison guide (when to use which); Go/No-Go decision framework (weighted criteria); Red flags checklist (accounting, legal, operational, HR) |
| **Tools** | AI business evaluator (upload financials, get preliminary analysis; basic = free, detailed = Pro); Valuation calculator (multiples-based); Target comparison tool (side-by-side evaluation) |
| **Timeline** | 2-4 weeks per target |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

---

## Phase 7: Letter of Intent (LOI) / Lettre de Cadrage

**"Making it official"**

The repreneur commits to a specific target by submitting a Lettre de Cadrage (LDC) or Letter of Intent (LOI). This document outlines proposed terms, price range, conditions, and requests an exclusivity period for due diligence.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Signed LOI/LDC with exclusivity period granted |
| **Competences** | LOI/LDC drafting; price range justification (based on Phase 6 valuation); condition precedent structuring; exclusivity negotiation tactics; multi-party coordination (advisors, seller, intermediary) |
| **Certification** | **LDC Validated** (Re-New reviews and approves Lettre de Cadrage before submission) [NOTE: already exists as milestone in current system] |
| **Resources to create** | LOI/LDC template (French legal standard with annotations); Key clauses explainer (exclusivity, conditions, timeline); Price justification methodology guide; Exclusivity period best practices (duration, obligations) |
| **Tools** | LDC builder (guided template with AI assistance); Document review workflow (submit to Re-New for review); Negotiation position tracker |
| **Timeline** | 2-4 weeks |
| **Status** | Milestone tracking: BUILT (LDC is an existing milestone). Builder tool: EMPTY |

## Phase 8: Due Diligence

**"What am I really buying?"**

The most intensive phase. The repreneur and advisory team conduct comprehensive due diligence across 6 workstreams: financial, legal, tax, operational, HR, and environmental. This is where deals die or get restructured.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | DD report covering all 6 workstreams; risk register; renegotiation points identified |
| **Competences** | Managing DD process and timeline; coordinating multiple advisors simultaneously; reading and interpreting DD reports; identifying deal-breakers vs. negotiation points; understanding GAP (garantie d'actif et de passif) implications |
| **Certification** | **DD Competent** (completed Re-New DD preparation course + passed assessment, or demonstrated through supervised DD process) |
| **Resources to create** | DD checklist by workstream (comprehensive, printable); "What to Look For" guides per workstream (financial, legal, tax, operational, HR, environmental); Common DD red flags (by industry); How to manage the DD timeline (45-60 days); DD cost guide (what to expect from advisors); "When to Walk Away" decision framework |
| **Tools** | DD checklist tracker (interactive, per workstream, with status indicators); Document vault (secure upload for DD documents); AI-powered DD question generator (based on target profile + financials) |
| **Timeline** | 45-60 days (typical) |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

## Phase 9: Negotiation & Deal Structuring

**"Getting to the right price and terms"**

Post-DD, the repreneur negotiates final terms. This involves price adjustment based on DD findings, GAP structuring, earn-out mechanics, seller financing, and coordinating all parties toward a signed protocole de cession (SPA).

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Draft protocole de cession / SPA with agreed terms |
| **Competences** | Principled negotiation (Fisher & Ury methodology); GAP structuring (duration: 3-5 years, cap: 50-100% of price, deductible, guarantee mechanisms); Earn-out design (performance metrics, duration, caps); Seller financing negotiation; Multi-party coordination (seller, advisors, banks, intermediary) |
| **Certification** | **Negotiation Ready** (completed negotiation workshop or demonstrated through successful deal history) |
| **Resources to create** | "Negotiation Tactics for Acquisition" guide; GAP structure explainer (duration, cap, deductible, guarantee types, escrow); Earn-out design guide (when to use, how to structure, pitfalls); SPA clause-by-clause walkthrough (French legal specifics); Deal structure comparison: asset purchase vs. share purchase |
| **Tools** | Deal term sheet builder; GAP calculator (model different scenarios); Negotiation position tracker |
| **Timeline** | 2-4 weeks |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

---

## Phase 10: Financing Finalization

**"Getting the money"**

Parallel to or following negotiation, the repreneur secures binding financing commitments. This involves bank presentations, holding company creation, BPI guarantee applications, and potentially investor coordination.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Binding commitment letters from lenders; holding company structure finalized; BPI guarantee secured (if applicable) |
| **Competences** | Bank presentation skills (French banks expect specific formats); Financial modeling for lending (business plan, cash flow projections); Holding company setup (SAS vs. SARL, tax implications); BPI France guarantee application process |
| **Certification** | **Financing Secured** (verified binding commitment letters uploaded and confirmed) |
| **Resources to create** | "Bank Presentation Template" guide (French banks expect specific format and content); Financial model templates (basic = free, advanced = Pro); Holding company setup guide (legal structure, costs, timeline); BPI France application walkthrough (step by step); "Investor Pitch Deck" template (for equity co-investors) |
| **Tools** | Financial model builder (AI-assisted for Pro tier); Bank submission tracker (which banks, status, terms offered); Document upload and verification |
| **Timeline** | 2-4 weeks |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

## Phase 11: Closing

**"Signing day"**

Legal ownership transfer. The culmination of 12-18 months of work. This phase is primarily administrative but emotionally significant and legally critical.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Legal ownership transfer completed; funds transferred; keys handed over |
| **Competences** | Understanding closing mechanics (conditions precedent verification); Final conditions check (all CPs satisfied); Fund transfer coordination (escrow, sequencing); Regulatory notifications (if applicable) |
| **Certification** | **Deal Closed** (Re-New verifies completed acquisition with proof of closing) |
| **Resources to create** | Closing day checklist (every step, every document); Post-signing administrative steps guide (URSSAF, CFE, banks, insurance); Day-one communication templates (employees, clients, suppliers) |
| **Tools** | Closing countdown tracker; Post-close task generator (automatic list of administrative tasks); Celebration workflow (Re-New acknowledgment and next steps) |
| **Timeline** | 1-2 weeks |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

---

## Phase 12: Transition & Handover

**"Taking the wheel"**

The seller hands over operational control. This is one of the most underestimated phases. The repreneur must build trust with the existing team while implementing their vision. The seller may remain for 3-12 months.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Operational independence from previous owner; team trust established; key relationships transferred |
| **Competences** | Change management; team communication (first meetings, vision sharing); supplier/client relationship transfer; knowledge capture from seller; pace-of-change management (too fast = resistance, too slow = momentum loss) |
| **Certification** | **Transition Complete** (Re-New confirms operational independence; this is a consulting touchpoint and relationship moment) |
| **Resources to create** | "First Meeting with Your New Team" script and guide; Knowledge transfer checklist (from seller to buyer, by function); Supplier/client communication templates; 30-60-90 day transition plan template; "Managing the Seller Relationship" guide |
| **Tools** | Transition task tracker; Seller handover schedule; Team pulse survey (anonymous feedback from acquired team) |
| **Timeline** | 3-12 months |
| **Status** | EMPTY |

---

## Phase 13: First 100 Days

**"Making it yours"**

The critical period that determines long-term success. Research shows 70-90% of M&A value destruction happens during integration. The repreneur must stabilize operations, achieve quick wins, establish credibility, and set strategic direction.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deliverable** | Stabilized operations; quick wins achieved; credibility established with team; strategic plan for Year 1 |
| **Competences** | Operational leadership; team motivation and development; performance monitoring; strategic planning; crisis management (inevitably something will go wrong); work-life balance management (burnout risk is real) |
| **Certification** | **Certified Repreneur** (completed the full Re-New journey; Re-New's highest individual certification; recognition of demonstrated capability through the entire lifecycle) |
| **Resources to create** | "First 100 Days Playbook" (comprehensive guide); Quick wins identification framework; Team assessment guide (who to keep, develop, or transition); Performance dashboard template; "When to Call for Help" guide (knowing limits); Alumni network access |
| **Tools** | First 100 days tracker (milestones and checkpoints); Performance dashboard (connects to business metrics); Re-New alumni community access; Ongoing coaching booking |
| **Timeline** | ~3 months |
| **Status** | EMPTY |
