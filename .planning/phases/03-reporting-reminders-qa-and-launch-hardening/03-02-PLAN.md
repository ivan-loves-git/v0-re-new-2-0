# 03-02: Freshness Display and Stale-Opportunity Reminder

## Goal

Make opportunity freshness visible to staff and add a simple reminder when an open opportunity has been in the system for at least 3 months without an active pursuit.

## Scope

- Show both exact date added and month added on opportunity rows.
- Keep the existing exact date on opportunity detail and make the month explicit.
- Add a staff-dashboard stale-opportunity panel.
- Treat active, paused, and draft opportunities as open for stale-reminder purposes.
- Exclude opportunities that already have an `active_pursuit` match.

## Out of Scope

- No email, Slack, or calendar reminder automation.
- No full M&A CRM follow-up workflow.
- No schema changes.
- No repreneur-facing freshness display.

## Implementation Steps

1. Add an opportunity freshness data action that reads existing opportunities and active-pursuit matches.
2. Build a dashboard panel using existing shadcn `Card`, `Alert`, `Badge`, `Button`, `Separator`, and `Table` primitives.
3. Insert the panel near the top of `/dashboard`, under the KPI section.
4. Update the opportunities table to show exact date and month added.
5. Update the opportunity detail header to make month added explicit.
6. Verify with build, focused typecheck filtering, and browser smoke on `/dashboard` and `/opportunities`.
7. Update GSD state and write the completion summary.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Opportunity rows show exact date added and month added.
- Staff dashboard lists open opportunities older than 90 days with no active pursuit.
- Opportunities with an active pursuit are not flagged stale.
- The reminder stays internal to staff dashboard routes.
- Build passes.
- Existing full typecheck baseline remains no worse for files touched in this plan.

## Executive Summary

This slice gives the team a simple operational memory: which opportunities are getting old and still do not have a validated repreneur pursuit. It helps Bertrand and the team decide what needs follow-up without creating a heavy CRM system.

The implementation is intentionally basic. It uses existing dates and match statuses, keeps everything internal, and avoids adding reminder automation until the June workflow is validated.
