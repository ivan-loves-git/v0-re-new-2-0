# Phase 2 Context: Core Deal Workflow

Phase 2 turns the opportunity foundation into the first usable deal workflow. The June version stays narrow: staff create structured match records, expose selected anonymized opportunities to matched repreneurs, collect interest/rejection, and then manage active pursuit stages.

## Scope Rules

- Repreneur access is limited to opportunities explicitly proposed to that repreneur.
- The portal uses the login email to match a Better Auth user to a `repreneurs.email` profile.
- Repreneurs do not get profile editing or advanced self-service in June.
- Staff-only opportunity fields, source/contact data, raw descriptions, and notes must stay out of repreneur-facing views.
- Matching remains structured/manual. AI interpretation stays deferred until the data model is validated.

## Phase 2 Plans

- 02-01: Structured matching and recommendation fields.
- 02-02: Repreneur opportunity access and anonymized opportunity detail.
- 02-03: Interest/reject actions and staff notification/review flow.
- 02-04: Validated pursuit, active-pursuit lock, and re-open logic.
- 02-05: Deal stage tracking from interest to closed/dropped.
- 02-06: Per-opportunity NDA and repreneur document download flow.
