## Executive Summary

This plan turns the repreneur profile from one overloaded page into a clear staff work surface. The page keeps the same header and existing data, then organizes the information into six tabs that match how the team thinks about the relationship.

The most important change is practical visibility: Overview becomes the daily command view, while Qualification restores the radar graphs and scoring content to a place the team can find immediately.
